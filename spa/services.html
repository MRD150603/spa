Full Body Traditional Thai Massage

Thai massage, also known as Thai yoga massage or Nuad Bo-Rarn, is a type of bodywork that originated in Thailand over 2,500 years ago. This massage is based on the principles of energy flow, known as “sen lines,” which are similar to the energy meridians used in traditional Chinese medicine.

Thai massage is typically performed on a mat on the floor, with the client fully clothed. The therapist uses a combination of acupressure, joint mobilization, and assisted yoga stretches to release tension and improve energy flow in the body. The therapist may use their hands, feet, elbows, and knees to apply pressure and manipulate the client’s body.

Full Body Swedish Massage

Swedish massage is a popular type of therapeutic massage that involves the use of long, gliding strokes, kneading, friction, and other techniques to promote relaxation and overall wellness. This type of massage was developed in Sweden in the 19th century and has since become one of the most widely practiced forms of massage in the world.

During a Swedish massage, the therapist will typically use massage oil or lotion to facilitate smooth, gliding strokes over the skin. The therapist may use a variety of techniques, including effleurage (long, sweeping strokes), petrissage (kneading and squeezing of the muscles), and tapotement (tapping or pounding motions). Swedish massage is typically a full-body massage that is designed to promote relaxation and improve circulation.

Full Body Vitalizing Balinese Massage

Balinese massage is a traditional healing practice that originated in Bali, Indonesia. It is a holistic approach to massage that focuses on balancing the body, mind, and spirit. Balinese massage is a combination of various massage techniques, including acupressure, deep tissue massage, reflexology, and aromamassage.

During a Balinese massage, the therapist will use a combination of techniques to promote deep relaxation and healing. The massage typically starts with gentle stretching and acupressure, followed by a deeper tissue massage to relieve muscle tension and knots. The therapist may also use hot stones, essential oils, and aromamassage to enhance the therapeutic effects of the massage

Full Body Revive Deep Tissue Massage

Deep tissue massage is a type of massage that focuses on realigning deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue. This type of massage is typically more intense than a Swedish massage, and the therapist will use slow, deep strokes to target specific areas of tension and pain.

During a deep tissue massage, the therapist may use their fingertips, knuckles, hands, elbows, and even forearms to apply deep pressure to the muscles and connective tissue. The pressure may be firm and intense, but the therapist will work with the client to ensure that the pressure is within their comfort level.
